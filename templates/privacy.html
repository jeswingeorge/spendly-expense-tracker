Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly when you create an account and use the Service, such as your name, email address, and password. We also collect the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may automatically collect limited technical information, such as your browser type and general usage activity, to help keep the Service running reliably.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to operate and maintain your account, display your expense history and spending summaries, and improve the features and reliability of the Service. We may also use your email address to send important account-related notices, such as security alerts or updates to these policies. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account details and expense records are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, after which your data will be removed from our active systems within a reasonable period.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on trusted third-party service providers for functions such as hosting, infrastructure, or email delivery, and these providers only process data as needed to perform those services on our behalf. We do not currently integrate with external banking or financial institutions.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the support channels listed in the app. We're happy to help clarify how your information is collected, used, or stored.
